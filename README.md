<h1 align="center">Local Stickers</h1>

An Android app to make WhatsApp stickers on your phone, with no account and no server.

- Pick a photo or a GIF from the gallery, from your files, or with Android's Share button.
- Crop, rotate and mirror it.
- Remove the background automatically (on the device), with an eraser and restore brush, or with a magic wand
  that removes a color.
- Add a white or black outline.
- Give the sticker a name and 1–3 emojis, and save it to a still or animated pack.
- Add the pack to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, and update it there after you change it.
- Export and import packs as a backup file.
